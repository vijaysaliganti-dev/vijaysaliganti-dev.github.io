PolySphere

Unity 6 mobile perspective puzzle

IN DEVELOPMENT
Unity 6C#Editor toolingGeometryiOS/Android
Play web prototype: Fox ↗
Play web prototype: Apple ↗
  • Artwork scatters into 3D fragments; one drag rotates the whole cluster, and only one viewpoint reassembles the picture. The animation at the top of this page uses the same method.
  • Wrote an Editor generator that cuts any image along its own facet lines into exact-count polygon pieces (421 pieces from one illustration), with UVs into the original art.
  • Scatter slides each piece along its camera ray, so the solved view stays pixel-exact under a perspective camera.
  • Tuned difficulty by measurement: swept every rotation axis, found and closed two "free solution" giveaways (roll symmetry and a silhouette cue), and auto-fit the camera so no piece leaves the screen on any phone aspect ratio.
  • Verified C# outside Unity with a mono test harness that renders the generator's real output.

ai systems

Smart NPC Opponent

Reinforcement-learning game AI in Panda3D

COMPLETE
Panda3DPyTorchStable Baselines3OpenAI GymLangGraphONNX
  • 3D fighting simulation where the enemy learns to attack, dodge and adapt to the player's patterns, trained with PPO.
  • Custom Gym environment with a shaped reward: +10 hit, +5 dodge, −10 hit taken, −5 cornered.
  • After 500,000 training steps the NPC's win rate rose from 12% to 78%.
  • LangGraph drives the NPC state machine (patrol, chase, attack, retreat) and LangChain writes context-aware dialogue. The trained model exports to .onnx for use in other engines.
Panda3D scene → Gym rewards → PPO training → LangGraph states → .onnx export
