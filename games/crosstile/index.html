How to play

  1. Every arrow points from a clue into the word it belongs to — right for across, down for down.
  2. Clues sit in the left and top margins, and on the bigger boards inside the grid too.
  3. A clue is a picture, or the word with letters missing. Words cross and share letters.
  4. You hold 5 letters at a time. Place one and the next is dealt from the pile.
  5. Tap a square, then tap a letter — or drag a tile onto the grid. Tap a placed letter to take it back.
  6. On a keyboard: type letters, ⌫ to undo, arrows to move, Tab to switch word.
